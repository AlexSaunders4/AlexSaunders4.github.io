The Bears' offensive line problem is a scheduling problem

12 August 2026

Open with the claim. One or two sentences that tell the reader what you think before you explain why — people decide whether to keep reading in about four seconds.

Then the argument. Keep paragraphs short enough to scan; the column here is set to roughly 64 characters, which is where reading speed peaks.

A subheading, when the piece turns

Use <h2> for section breaks inside a long piece. Links look like this.

Pull quotes and block quotes sit here. Use them for someone else's words, not to re-state your own.
